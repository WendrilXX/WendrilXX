## About

I am a Full Stack Developer who turns ideas into end-to-end digital products — from interface to infrastructure. I build clear, fast, and maintainable experiences while making deliberate decisions around architecture, security, and performance.

My experience spans web and mobile applications, APIs, databases, automation, and containerized environments. I like to understand the problem before choosing the technology and keep every solution simple enough to evolve.

Part of my recent experience also comes from private products: end-to-end web systems, backend services, and mobile applications built with reliability and maintainability in mind.
